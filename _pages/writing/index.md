# Writing

Things I write when a question refuses to leave me alone.

Not everything here is research. Some are essays, some are observations, and some are simply ideas I wanted to put somewhere.

## Recent

### On Being Curious

I think curiosity is a strange kind of discipline.

It rarely begins with knowing what you are looking for. Sometimes it begins with noticing something that feels slightly wrong, slightly interesting, or simply unexplained.

A good question can be more useful than a good answer.

---

### The Internet as a Notebook

The internet is often treated as a place to publish finished things.

I prefer to think of it as a notebook.

A notebook can contain half-formed ideas, questions, diagrams, failed experiments, and things that may become useful later.

Maybe a personal website can work the same way.

---

### Notes on AI

The interesting question about AI may not be whether machines can think like humans.

It may be what happens to humans when machines begin thinking alongside them.

That distinction changes the question completely.
